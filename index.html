01 The problem

The platform pays one account. Your team is several people.

And the number moves after you’ve already told everyone what they’re getting.

A

The estimate isn’t final

Creator Portal shows a figure, then posts the real one roughly 30 days after the period closes. They don’t have to match.

B

Spreadsheets get edited

Someone fixes a formula by hand in November. In February nobody can reconstruct why September changed.

C

Then you’re the one chasing

You did the maths, you absorb the argument, and every January someone asks you for their earnings for tax.

02 How it works

Agree it once. The ledger does the rest.

Set the split per place

Collaborators differ on each island, so splits belong to an island rather than the team. Shares can start late or sunset.

Everyone signs off

A period only locks when every named contributor has approved. Nobody can set their own share.

Post the platform’s figures

Provisional first, then the final. The difference is carried into next month, never clawed back.

Everyone sees their own account

Running balance, what’s been paid, and an export their accountant can actually use.

03 Why not a spreadsheet

A locked period cannot quietly change.

That’s the whole product. The maths is the easy part.

Estimates are reversed, not edited

When the final arrives, the estimate is withdrawn on the record and the settled figure booked. You can always show what was believed and when it changed.

Nobody is asked to send money back

A downward revision becomes a negative adjustment on next month. Clawing back cash someone already spent is how these arrangements end.

Every split is versioned and signed

September reconciles against the split September locked with. Later changes create a new version and leave the old one intact.

07 Questions

The things people ask first.

Something not answered here? Email isola@sundappleapp.com and a person replies.

Does Isola hold or move my money?

No. The platform pays you, you pay your team through your own bank, and Isola keeps the record. There is no float, no custody, and no cut of the payout.

What happens when a payout is revised after I have told everyone?

The provisional credit is reversed and replaced by the final figure rather than edited, and the difference carries into the next month. Nobody is ever asked to send money back.

Can the team owner quietly change a figure?

No. Statements are frozen when posted, so a September statement reads identically in December. Every split version, approval, figure and payment is recorded with who did it and when, and nothing can be removed.

Can a contributor check the books themselves?

Yes. Any team member can run the books check, not just the owner. A verification only the owner can run verifies nothing.

Which platforms does Isola support?

Fortnite islands, Roblox experiences, Minecraft servers and maps, and FiveM servers. A team can run places on more than one platform and keep one set of books.

Does it cost more to add collaborators?

No. Pricing follows the volume your team handles, not how many people are on it. Everyone gets an account on every plan, including free.

Is anything locked behind a paid plan?

Nothing your collaborators need. Every contributor can see, check and export their own records on every plan, free. Paid plans add conveniences for whoever runs the team — scheduled statements, a year-end pack, automatic exchange rates, more than one owner and API access.

Can Isola pay my team for me?

No, and it never will. Isola produces a file listing what each person is owed, which you upload to your own bank and authorise yourself. CSV for most banks, NACHA for the United States, SEPA for the eurozone, ABA for Australia. The file contains no bank account details.

Can I move an existing team onto Isola?

Yes. Record what each person was owed on the day you start, and the books begin from there rather than pretending nobody is owed anything.

What can I send my accountant?

A general ledger of every transaction, and a reconciliation showing opening, recognised, paid and closing figures per person. Both take a date range and download as Excel, CSV or JSON — and both are on the read-only link you give them, so they need not ask you.

Isola records. It never moves money.
