Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

1. Who we are

Isola is provided by Ibrahim Ahmed, a sole trader trading as Isola, in London, United Kingdom. He is the controller of the personal data described here under UK GDPR. Contact: isola@sundappleapp.com

2. What we hold

  • Account: your name, email address, and a hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Financial: the splits your team agreed, payout figures entered, expenses, advances, opening balances, and what has been paid.
  • Payment references: if you record a payment, whatever reference you type. We suggest a bank reference; we do not ask for and do not want account numbers.
  • Audit trail: who did what in a team and when — approvals, postings, payments, exports. This is part of the product: it is how a team can show nothing was changed quietly.
  • Chat apps: your Discord or Telegram user ID and the language you chose for the bot, only if you link an account. Isola reads only commands and messages sent to it or that mention it; it cannot read your other messages, your servers, or your member lists.
  • Google: if you link a Google account to sign in, its account identifier and email address.
  • Notices: which version of these policies you were last told about, so you are told about a change exactly once.
  • Technical: a session cookie so you stay signed in, and server logs, which include IP addresses, kept for a short period for security.

We do not hold tax identification numbers, bank account details, or identity documents, and we do not hold card details.

Payment files

A payment file contains each contributor’s name, the amount they are owed, and a payment reference — data you already hold in Isola, arranged in a format your bank reads.

No bank account details are involved. Isola has never asked for a sort code, account number or IBAN, does not store them, and does not put them in the file. Those fields are left for you or your bank to complete.

We record that a file was downloaded, by whom, how many people it covered and the total, in the team’s audit trail alongside every other action. We do not keep a copy of the file itself.

3. Why we hold it, and on what basis

  • To run the service you signed up for — performance of a contract.
  • To keep the shared record accurate for everyone on a team, to keep the service secure, and to prevent abuse — our legitimate interests, and those of the other people on your team.
  • To keep financial records for as long as the law requires — legal obligation.

Nothing here is used for advertising or profiling, no decision about you is made automatically, and we do not sell your data or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

4. Who else sees it

  • Your team. The owner sees balances owed across the team. Contributors see only their own account. One contributor cannot see another's statement.
  • Anyone your team owner gives a read-only books link to, such as an accountant. That link never shows an individual contributor's balance.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract: Fly.io, which hosts the application and database; Resend, which sends our emails; Stripe, which takes subscription payments and handles card details we never see; and Cloudflare, which hosts this website and runs the check that a sign-up is a person and not a bot.
  • Discord and Telegram, if you use the bots. Commands and replies pass through their servers and are handled under their own privacy policies.
  • Epic Games and Roblox, only when you look up a place: the place's code or name is sent to their public API. Nothing about you is sent.

Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

5. Where it lives

The application and its database run in London. Some of the providers above are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK's approved contract clauses.

6. Keeping and deleting it

This is the part worth reading properly, because there is a real tension and we would rather set it out than gloss over it.

Isola's value comes from the ledger being append-only. Entries are never edited or deleted; a correction is a new entry that reverses an old one. That is what makes it possible to show what your team agreed in September when you are arguing about it in December. That sits awkwardly with the right to erasure, so here is exactly what happens.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your account screen.

What is destroyed: your name, email address, password, sessions, and any linked Google, Discord or Telegram account.

What is kept: the figures on your team’s books, without your name on them. Two reasons. A team’s accounts only reconcile when everyone’s figures are present, so removing yours would make your former colleagues’ statements wrong. And UK law requires business records to be kept, which Article 17(3)(b) of the UK GDPR permits as an exception to erasure.

After deletion, the entries show an anonymous placeholder. You cannot be identified from them, and the same placeholder is not reused, so you cannot be recognised across teams.

If you are the only owner of a team that still has other people in it, you will be asked to make somebody else an owner first. Otherwise they would lose access to their own records.

Deleting a team

A team owner can delete a team entirely. Isola refuses while anyone is still owed money, because deleting the record of a debt is not housekeeping. Everyone else on the team is emailed once it is done, and their own accounts are unaffected.

How long

Financial records are kept for as long as required for accounting purposes, currently six years, then deleted. Server logs are kept for a short period. Account details are kept until you delete your account.

If the trade-off above is not acceptable to you, export your statement and close your account before you have anything credited.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, correction of anything wrong, deletion as described above, a portable export, or that we restrict or stop using your data in a particular way. A copy of everything we hold about you downloads from your account screen, and your statement of account exports as Excel, CSV or JSON from your own page, on any plan, at any time, including if the team stops paying. For anything else, email us and we will answer within a month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, tell us first, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Children

Isola is not for children under 13. If we learn we hold an account for a child under 13, we will delete it. Contributors aged 13 to 17 should use Isola with a parent or guardian's agreement.

9. Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and Secure. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising cookie, which is why there is no cookie banner. Fonts are served from our own site rather than a third party's, so loading a page does not send your address to anyone else.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sign-in attempts are rate limited. Everything travels over HTTPS. Sessions expire and are invalidated when you reset your password.

No system is perfect. If we ever have a breach affecting your data we will tell you and the ICO within the time the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change anything material here we will tell you by email rather than quietly updating the page.
