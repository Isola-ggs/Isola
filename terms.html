Payment files

Isola can produce a file listing what each contributor is owed, in a format your bank accepts for bulk payments — CSV, NACHA for the United States, SEPA for the eurozone, or ABA for Australia. The file is a list until you choose to upload it to your own bank, from your own account, and authorise the payments yourself. Nothing in Isola initiates a payment, so Isola is not a payment service and is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Payment files contain no bank account details. Isola has never asked for a sort code, account number or IBAN and does not store them.

Check a file before you use it. It is produced from the figures recorded in your team, and those figures come from you. Isola shows the total and every line before you download it. You are responsible for what you upload to your bank and for the payments you authorise.
