1. What Isola is

Isola is record-keeping software. It records what your team agreed each contributor's share would be, what was posted for a period, and what has been paid. That is the whole of it.

Isola is not an accountant, a bookkeeper, or a financial adviser. Nothing it produces is financial advice, and no output should be treated as a professional opinion on your affairs.

2. Isola never holds or moves your money

Epic pays you. You pay your collaborators through your own bank. Isola writes down that it happened.

No funds pass through us at any point. We are not a payment service and are not authorised as one. If you are ever asked to send money to Isola for onward payment to anyone, that is not us.

Payment files

Isola can produce a file listing what each contributor is owed, in a format your bank accepts for bulk payments. The file is a list until you choose to upload it to your own bank, from your own account, and authorise the payments yourself. Nothing in Isola initiates a payment, so Isola is not a payment service and is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Payment files contain no bank account details. Isola has never asked for a sort code, account number or IBAN and does not store them.

Check a file before you use it. It is produced from the figures recorded in your team, and those figures come from you. Isola shows the total and every line before you download it. You are responsible for what you upload to your bank and for the payments you authorise.

3. You are responsible for what you enter

Isola calculates accurately from the figures you give it. It has no way to know whether those figures are the right ones.

  • You are responsible for entering payout figures correctly.
  • You are responsible for the splits your team agrees.
  • You are responsible for what you file with any tax authority.

We will fix defects in the software. We cannot be responsible for a figure that was typed in wrongly.

4. Accounts and access

Team owners can invite contributors, post figures, and change the plan. Contributors can always see and export their own statement of account.

Access to your own records is never withheld for non-payment. If a team's plan lapses, posting new figures pauses. Reading and exporting what is already recorded does not, on any plan, in any state. This is a commitment, not a current courtesy.

5. Plans and payment

Plans are priced on the payout volume a team handles, averaged over the trailing twelve months, and not on how many contributors are on the team.

Prices are shown before you subscribe. We will give reasonable notice before changing them. You can cancel at any time; access to existing records continues as described above.

6. Availability

We aim to keep Isola available and will take reasonable care with backups, but we do not offer a guaranteed uptime commitment at this stage. Your records are exportable at any time and you should keep your own copies of anything you rely on.

7. Ending the arrangement

You can close a team at any time. Before you do, export what you need — we will keep records for a reasonable period afterwards but not indefinitely.

We may suspend an account that is being used unlawfully or that puts other users at risk. We will tell you why, and you will be able to export your records.
